About us

A small forecourt, held to a high standard

FF Motorhouse is an independent dealership just outside Manchester. We buy fewer cars than the big sites and spend longer on each one — checking history, road-testing properly, and turning away anything we wouldn't hand to a member of our own family.

Every vehicle arrives prepared: fresh service, fresh MOT, valeted, and photographed in daylight rather than a lighting booth. Part exchange is welcome, finance can be arranged through our partners, and the price on the windscreen is the price you pay.

  • HPI checked, every car Clear finance, no write-off history, mileage verified.
  • Serviced and MOT'd before sale Twelve months' MOT and a full service on handover.
  • No admin fees The advertised price is the price. Nothing added at the desk.
